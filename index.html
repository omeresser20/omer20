Omer B20 (English)

Omer B20 is a digital subscription service: a WhatsApp bot that connects passengers and approved local drivers in Omer, Israel. Subscribers get access to the bot's ride-matching service. Ride fares are paid directly between passenger and driver and are not part of the subscription.

Plans: Passenger: 3-week free trial, then ₪30 per 30 days. Driver: 14-day free trial, then ₪30 per 14 days or ₪40 per 30 days.

Renewal & cancellation: Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled anytime, effective at the end of the paid period.

Refunds: Subscription payments are non-refundable. Cancelling stops the next charge; access continues until the end of the paid period. Subject to applicable law.
